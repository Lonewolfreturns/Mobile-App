# Flux Chamber Hub — Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026 · Innovative Waste Management (IWM)

> Host this page at a public web address. Google Play and the App Store both
> ask for a link to it. The same text is shown inside the app under
> Settings → About & Privacy (`src/legal.ts`) — keep the two in step.

## What this app does

Flux Chamber Hub is a control panel for flux chamber instruments. It connects
over your local WiFi network to the chambers at the addresses you enter in
Settings, shows their sensor readings, sends them the commands you choose, and
copies their measurement files onto your device.

## Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no advertising,
analytics, crash reporting or tracking software, and it does not send any
information to us or to anyone else.

## Information kept on your device

The app stores your settings (the chamber address range and timing values) and
the measurement files you copy from your chambers. These stay on your device. A
file only leaves the device when you choose to share it using your phone or
tablet's own share menu.

## Network use

The app communicates only with devices on your local network, using private
network addresses. It refuses to contact any other address and makes no
connections to the internet. The chambers speak plain (unencrypted) HTTP, so
use the app on a WiFi network you trust.

## Permissions

Local network access (iOS) and network access (Android) are used only to reach
your chambers. Vibration is used only for touch feedback. The app does not ask
for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or any other personal
information.

## Demo mode

Demo mode shows simulated chambers so the app can be tried without hardware. In
demo mode the app does not use the network at all.

## Children

The app is a professional instrument tool and is not directed at children. It
collects no information from anyone.

## Deleting your data

You can delete any copied data file inside the app. Uninstalling the app
removes its settings and every file it saved.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with a new effective
date.
